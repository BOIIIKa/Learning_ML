<Slide lang="en" part="method" layout="center" title="One step on a quadratic bowl" say="Start with the simplest landscape: a quadratic.">

<Morph>

<Step effect="morph" say="One step of gradient descent.">

$$
x_{t+1} = x_t - \eta \nabla f(x_t)
$$

</Step>

<Step effect="morph" say="A quadratic's gradient is H times x minus x star.">

$$
x_{t+1} - x^* = (I - \eta H)(x_t - x^*)
$$

</Step>

<Step effect="morph" say="Along each eigen-direction of H, the error is multiplied by one minus eta lambda.">

$$
e_{t+1} = (1 - \eta\lambda)\, e_t
$$

</Step>

</Morph>

<Step effect="focus" say="The steep direction needs eta below two over L; the flat one shrinks only a little per step.">
<p class="big-q sm">Stable: $\eta \lt 2/L$　Speed: set by $\kappa = L/\mu$</p>
</Step>

</Slide>

<Slide lang="en" part="math" layout="center" title="Linear convergence" say="And for a general smooth, strongly convex function? Two inequalities are enough.">

<Morph>

<Step effect="morph" say="The descent lemma: with step one over L, each step drops at least the squared gradient over two L.">

$$
f(x_{t+1}) \le f(x_t) - \frac{1}{2L}\|\nabla f(x_t)\|^2
$$

</Step>

<Step effect="morph" say="Strong convexity bounds the gradient from below.">

$$
\|\nabla f(x_t)\|^2 \ge 2\mu\,\big(f(x_t) - f^*\big)
$$

</Step>

<Step effect="morph" say="Together: each step multiplies the gap by one minus mu over L.">

$$
f(x_{t+1}) - f^* \le \Big(1 - \frac{\mu}{L}\Big)\big(f(x_t) - f^*\big)
$$

</Step>

</Morph>

<Step say="Shrinking by a factor epsilon takes about kappa times log one over epsilon steps.">Steps $\approx \kappa \ln(1/\varepsilon)$</Step>

</Slide>

<Slide lang="en" part="method" layout="center" title="Momentum: a damped heavy ball" say="Give the ball some inertia.">

<Morph>

<Step effect="morph" say="A ball with mass and friction, rolling down the slope.">

$$
m \ddot x + c \dot x + \nabla f(x) = 0
$$

</Step>

<Step effect="morph" say="Discretise with step h.">

$$
x_{t+1} = x_t - \frac{h^2}{m}\nabla f(x_t) + \Big(1 - \frac{ch}{m}\Big)(x_t - x_{t-1})
$$

</Step>

<Step effect="morph" say="That is the heavy-ball method, with eta equal to h squared over m and beta equal to one minus c h over m.">

$$
x_{t+1} = x_t - \eta \nabla f(x_t) + \beta (x_t - x_{t-1})
$$

</Step>

</Morph>

<Step say="Velocity builds up along consistent directions, one over one minus beta times; back-and-forth swings cancel.">Rate $\frac{\kappa - 1}{\kappa + 1} \to \frac{\sqrt\kappa - 1}{\sqrt\kappa + 1}$</Step>

</Slide>

<Slide lang="en" part="math" layout="center" title="Adam's bias correction" say="Adam uses moving averages of the gradient. But they start at zero.">

<Morph>

<Step effect="morph" say="Unroll the first-moment recursion.">

$$
m_t = (1 - \beta_1) \sum_{i=1}^{t} \beta_1^{t-i} g_i
$$

</Step>

<Step effect="morph" say="If every gradient has mean g, the sum is geometric.">

$$
\mathbb E\, m_t = (1 - \beta_1^t)\, g
$$

</Step>

<Step effect="morph" say="So divide by one minus beta one to the t. The same for the second moment.">

$$
\hat m_t = \frac{m_t}{1 - \beta_1^t}, \qquad \hat v_t = \frac{v_t}{1 - \beta_2^t}
$$

</Step>

</Morph>

<Step say="Without the correction the first step is 3.16 times the learning rate; with it, exactly the learning rate.">First step: $3.16\,\eta \to \eta$</Step>

</Slide>

<Slide lang="en" part="method" layout="center" title="Adam and AdamW" say="How big are Adam's steps?">

<Step say="m hat over root v hat is of order one: each parameter moves about eta per step, whatever the scale of its gradient.">$\theta \leftarrow \theta - \eta\, \hat m / (\sqrt{\hat v} + \epsilon)$: about $\eta$ per step</Step>

<Step say="Put L2 into Adam and the penalty is divided by root v too: parameters with large gradients barely decay.">Adam + L2: decay diluted by $\sqrt{\hat v}$</Step>

<Step effect="focus" say="AdamW moves the decay out of the ratio: every step multiplies by one minus eta lambda.">
<p class="big-q sm">AdamW: $\theta \leftarrow \theta - \eta\big(\hat m/\sqrt{\hat v} + \lambda\theta\big)$</p>
</Step>

</Slide>
