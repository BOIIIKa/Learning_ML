### Traps

- **Underflow**: $\alpha$ decays exponentially with length and becomes 0 within a few hundred steps. Either rescale at every step or work in log space throughout (log-sum-exp).
- **Local maxima**: Baum–Welch is sensitive to its starting point. Use several random restarts and keep the best likelihood; pick the number of states by BIC or held-out likelihood.
- **What the states mean**: state numbers learned without labels are arbitrary (label switching); interpret them afterwards from their emission distributions.
- **Match the decoder to the metric**: for per-position accuracy use the posterior maximum ($\gamma$); for whole-sequence correctness use Viterbi; for entity-level F1, Viterbi usually wins.
- **Try a sliding window plus a plain classifier first**: sometimes that is enough; structured decoding pays most when labels are strongly dependent (sticky states, grammatical constraints).
