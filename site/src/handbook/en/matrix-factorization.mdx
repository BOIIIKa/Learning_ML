### Traps

- **Standardize first**: with mixed units the first component is just "the feature with the largest units".
- **Put it in the pipeline**: PCA is learned from data too; fit it on the training set only, or cross-validation leaks information.
- **`n_components` can be a fraction**: `PCA(0.95)` picks enough components for 95% of the variance; `svd_solver="randomized"` is much faster on large matrices.
- **Use TruncatedSVD for sparse data**: PCA centres the data and turns a sparse matrix dense; LSA on text uses `TruncatedSVD`.
- **High variance is not the same as useful**: PCA ignores the labels, and the direction of greatest variance may be irrelevant to the target; with labels, consider PLS or plain regularized regression.
- **t-SNE / UMAP are for looking only**: cluster sizes and distances between clusters cannot be interpreted, and results depend on `perplexity` and the random seed.
