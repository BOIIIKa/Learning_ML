- **Stationary distribution**: $\pi^\top P = \pi^\top$; irreducible + aperiodic ⇒ unique, the law of $X_t$ converges to $\pi$, and time averages $\to \mathbb E_\pi f$ (ergodic theorem).
- **Detailed balance**: $\pi(x)T(x \to x') = \pi(x')T(x' \to x)$ ⇒ $\pi$ is stationary (sum over $x$).
- **Metropolis–Hastings**: accept with $\min\!\big(1, \frac{\tilde\pi(x')q(x \mid x')}{\tilde\pi(x)q(x' \mid x)}\big)$; the normalizing constant cancels; on rejection record the current point again. Tune a random-walk step to about 0.2–0.5 acceptance; discard the burn-in.
- **Gibbs sampling**: draw each $x_j$ from $\pi(x_j \mid x_{-j})$ in turn; acceptance is always 1.
- **PageRank**: $r = \frac{1-d}{n}\mathbf 1 + dMr$, the random surfer's stationary distribution; Perron–Frobenius gives existence and uniqueness; power iteration converges like $|\lambda_2|^t$ with $|\lambda_2| \le d$.
- **Examples**: A→B,C; B→C; C→A, $d = 0.85$ ⇒ $r = (686, 380, 703)/1769 \approx (0.388, 0.215, 0.397)$; for an $N(0,1)$ target, proposing 1 from 0 is accepted with probability $e^{-1/2} \approx 0.607$.
- **LDA by collapsed Gibbs**: $P(z_i = k \mid \cdot) \propto (n_{dk}^{-i} + \alpha)\frac{n_{kw}^{-i} + \beta}{n_k^{-i} + V\beta}$, three count tables only; use online variational inference for large corpora.
